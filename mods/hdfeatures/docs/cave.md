# HD Features: Cave of Shadows

The Cave of Shadows part of **HD Features** (`local.ricky.hdfeatures`, `mods/hdfeatures`). It began as its own mod, Cave of Shadows (`local.ricky.caveofshadows`); see the repository `README.md` for the install and `BUILDING.md` for the build (each option is explained by its tooltip in the mod's panel). The sources are `mods/hdfeatures/src/cave/` (namespace `cave`).

Adds Twilight Princess HD's Cave of Shadows (stage `D_SB11`) to Dusklight v2.0.3. The stage files, HD's text and the amiibo art are not in the mod. At the first start, the mod makes them on your PC from your own TP HD, into Dusklight's data folder (`mod_data\local.ricky.hdfeatures\files-<id>\`). Those game files stay on your PC: never share them. The `.dusk` itself has no game files in it. Paths like `res/cave/...` and `overlay/res/...` below are inside that folder. The Mods panel's **Cave of Shadows** switch turns it off and on again without a reload (inside the cave you leave it first, as with **Leave the cave**).

## Entering and leaving, like in HD

- **Virtual Wolf Link amiibo.** Once Faron's light spirit is restored, open the pause menu and go to the Collection screen. An amiibo prompt (Wolf Link, the amiibo logo and the button) appears in the corner for about 3 seconds, once per visit to the menu; press **Z** (option "Amiibo button", `cave-amiibo_button`: Z, X or Y). No figure is needed. The prompt is only ever visible on the Collection screen itself: it fades out as soon as the menu closes or a sub-window or the scan screen opens.
  - A scan screen recreates HD's amiibo popup (parchment, NFC glow, the Wolf Link figure) with HD's own text.
  - **Allowed:** the Collection screen fades out and HD's **"Cave of Shadows" screen** fades in, built from HD's own save-menu layouts (`res/cave/confirm/`, see below): the title bar, the Confirm (A) / Back (B) hints, the gold plate with the Wolf Link emblem showing the hearts stored in the amiibo and "Level B*n*" (the floor your last stored run ended on), and the buttons **Save and begin** / **Cancel**, with HD's music for this screen. Left/Right moves between the two buttons.
    - **Save and begin:** the game's own save screen opens (the same one as the Collection screen's Save: pick a Quest Log, confirm an overwrite, "Saving..."). It ends with HD's "Saved." (not GC's "Saved. Continue playing?"), and then, as in HD, the stage changes straight from the save screen: "Saved." stays up while the game fades to black, and you arrive in the cave. Nothing happens to Link or Midna in between, no warp and no sound, exactly as in HD (read from HD's `Zelda.rpx`: the save's last step calls the cave entry in the same frame, and the menu then just stays drawn until the scene changes). That works anywhere the amiibo does, Castle Town included. Nothing happens until the save is written: backing out of the save screen (B) or a save error takes you back to the Collection screen, as in HD. Only that save screen's own write counts (an autosave never starts a trip). The save is made before anything about the trip changes, so loading it puts you back where its save point is.
    - **Not in HD, off by default:** with **Midna's warp-out on entry** (`cave-entry_warp`) on, the menu closes first and Midna's portal warp takes you in (as in GC's map warp; a human Link turns into a wolf first), where GC allows a map warp (not while Midna is gravely ill); elsewhere (dungeons, houses, caves, or Castle Town as a human) HD's way above is used.
    - **Cancel** or **B:** back to the Collection screen.
    - Before the amiibo has stored anything, the screen shows the centred title bar without a title and without the plate. That follows HD's code for a new figure: its layout keeps that title text hidden and no HD code shows it (inferred; not compared with footage of a first scan).
    - The first time in a session, the screen's files (about 11 MB) are read while the screen is black between the Collection screen and the "Cave of Shadows" screen, so any pause is hidden by the fade.
  - You always arrive on floor 1 as Wolf Link with full hearts. Every entry looks the same, as in HD: a fade-in, the wolf (Midna on his back) trots forward off the entrance ledge, and the gate closes behind him (room 19's own player entry, start mode 5; HD's entry sends the same values as the mod, and its "0xCA" start parameter only goes into the turn-restart record, so there is no warp-in). Like in HD, the arrival cutscene and the voice of the cave only come on an entry that brings something new: the very first entry (HD message 5060), and the first entry after a deeper section has opened (HD message 5061). Every other entry starts without them. Floors 7 and 21 play a short camera intro (no text) whenever you walk in.
  - **Where it works, as in HD:** everywhere except HD's own short list below. So it also works in dungeons (Hyrule Castle included), houses and shops, caves and grottos, in the twilight, and while riding Epona, swimming, climbing or carrying something. HD's check (read from HD's `Zelda.rpx`) refuses only:
    - Ganondorf's stages (`D_MN09A`, `D_MN09B`, `D_MN09C`) and `F_SP200`.
    - The boss and mini-boss rooms of every dungeon (a `D_MN*` stage entered in room 50-52), Zant's throne room (`D_MN08A`) and the Zant fight (`D_MN08D`).
    - Zora's River (`F_SP112`) when entered in room 1, where Iza is.
    - In a canoe at the fishing pond or Lake Hylia, while taming a horse, during the Kakariko bow and arrow game, while gliding with a Cucco over Lake Hylia, during sumo, on the Snowpeak ice-leaf slide, and in the Sacred Grove during the Skull Kid chase (inferred).
    - While a timer runs (races, timed deliveries and other timed challenges).
  - **Not allowed:** HD's message 1530 (a short "not here" refusal), the same for every case above; the Collection screen stays open. Before Faron's light spirit is restored the amiibo does nothing, as in HD.
  - **While Midna is gravely ill** (after the Lakebed Temple, until Zelda revives her) the amiibo still takes you in, as in HD, but floor 1's arrival sends you straight back with a Withdraw. Midna's warp-out does not play then (GC never warps while she is ill); you hear HD's two warp sounds and the screen fades.
- **Inside the cave:**
  - Calling Midna opens HD's cave menu: Withdraw, Talk to Midna, and (with the Shadow Crystal) "Transform into human", which Midna refuses as in HD. Quick transform is refused with an error sound.
  - **Withdraw, as in HD:** HD's Withdraw (event 44) plays two warp sounds and then resets to the title screen, so the player reloads the save "Save and begin" wrote. On PC that reset closes Dusklight, so the mod gives you the same end state without it: once the talk is over, Midna's warp-out plays (the same warp as **Leave the cave**), then you arrive at **that save's return place** (where loading it would put you: the area of the last save point you passed, not necessarily where you scanned) with that save's hearts (at least 3), rupees, items, flags and form. Nothing from the visit is kept: rupees and items found in the cave, the cave's "new path" announcements and the visit's run record are all undone, exactly as after the reload. If you saved again from the pause menu inside the cave, Withdraw goes back to that newer save instead (the save a reload would pick). A floor-41 reward that is still waiting to be handed out is handed out first and then undone with the rest. The save is put back in memory at the stage change; the next save or autosave writes it to your file again. Where the warp-out cannot play (in the air or water, Midna not on your back, Midna gravely ill, or **Midna's portal warp** off) you hear HD's two warp sounds and the screen fades instead. With **Withdraw like HD** (`cave-hd_withdraw`) off, Withdraw instead returns you to where you entered (the exact spot after an entry from a field; after an entry from anywhere else, the save's return place, as HD would), in your original form, with full hearts and the rupees you entered with, keeping what you found. The panel's **Leave the cave** button always does the latter, and so does Withdraw after the debug **Enter the cave now** button (that way in does not save, so there is no save to go back to).
  - **Exit portals** (floors 6, 20 and 41; 6/20 only while the next section is locked, see below) work as in HD:
    - The portal is invisible until its switch turns on, then appears at once (one burst of light, no appear cutscene), and Midna gets off your back and waits at it.
    - Step into it and Midna asks whether you want to go outside (HD's question; a second wording once you have seen the Master Sword cutscene, HD message 1335), Yes / No. **No:** you walk back out; stepping in again asks again.
    - **Yes:** the wolf runs into the middle of the portal, the camera closes in, the portal's column of light grows and you dissolve upward in the warp effect. Leaving this way counts the section as cleared, keeps the rupees you collected and refills the cave's chests.
    - Then the **"Cave of Shadows Record"** screen opens over the frozen cave (HD's save-menu screen, drawn from HD's own layouts): the amiibo plate first shows the hearts stored in your virtual Wolf Link amiibo and counts them up to your current hearts (with the difference and "NEW Record!" when you have more), then the "amiibo used" row, the completion time, the max combo and the damage received count up from your previous record; values that beat it turn gold. A skips the counting.
    - If you have more hearts than the amiibo holds, it asks whether to store them in the Wolf Link amiibo (HD message 1261). **Yes** writes the floor, your hearts and your heart containers to the amiibo (a short NFC popup, HD message 1567; the first time with HD's long explanation of what stored hearts do, 1568, afterwards 1569). **No** asks whether to leave without saving (1262; No or B goes back to the question). Otherwise there is only a **Close** button.
    - Every way out then opens the game's save screen (pick a Quest Log, overwrite?, "Saving...", HD's "Saved."; the cursor starts on the Quest Log you play), the menu closes and you go back to your **save's return place** (the area you last entered, where loading the save would put you), in your original form, with full hearts and the rupees you left the cave with. As in HD the return is requested while the screen is still black; the menu's own fade-in then shows the empty cave for a moment before the black fade to the world (HD's menu fades in the same way here; that its fader then behaves like GC's is inferred). B in the Quest Log list skips the save (the record and the amiibo data are kept and written with your next save).
  - Falling in battle and choosing **Continue** starts the run again on floor 1 with full hearts, as in HD: the floors' gates close again, the chests refill and your rupees go back to what you entered with. ("Quit" is the game's own reset to the title screen; the mod does not change it. On PC that reset path ends in `OSRestart`, which is what closed Dusklight when the mod still copied HD's soft-reset Withdraw, so "Quit" may close the game too; unchecked.) A plain void-out restarts the floor, like the GC Cave of Ordeals.
- **HD sections** ("HD sections" option, `cave-hd_sections`, on by default). The cave has three sections, and every visit starts on floor 1, whatever you have unlocked. What the story has unlocked only changes how floors 5/6 and 19/20 behave:
  - **Next section still locked:** clearing floor 5 (or 19) ends the run. The voice of the cave tells you to come back when you are stronger (HD message 5066; 5067 once you have done everything the story needs), then the gate opens to the rest floor 6 (or 20). There, standing in the middle makes the portal appear (the chest is there too); the gate further down stays shut. Leaving through that portal clears the section.
  - **Next section unlocked:** floor 5 (or 19) opens straight away with no talk, and on floor 6 (or 20) standing in the middle opens the gate down instead (no portal). The run goes on.

  | Section | Floors | Opens when |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 1–5 (rest floor 6) | Faron's light spirit is restored (`M_019`): the amiibo works from then on |
  | 2 | 7–19 (rest floor 20) | You have left through the floor-6 portal **and** seen the Mirror Chamber scene after Arbiter's Grounds (`F_0685`) |
  | 3 | 21–40 (floor 41) | You have left through the floor-20 portal **and** the Sols have powered the Master Sword in the Palace of Twilight (HD's own flag; in GC the Light Sword's item bits stand in for it) |

  So a section never opens by itself: you finish the previous one at its portal first, and the next entry is the one that announces the new path (5061). HD also says so on the Collection screen, once (message 1546, and 1545 the first time the amiibo can be used); here those are notices while the Collection screen is up.

  Turn the option off to play all 41 floors in one run (no talk on floors 5/19, no portals on 6/20).
- **Record time:** like HD's hidden cave timer, the run's time starts when you drop from the ledge into the floor-1 arena and stops at the floor-5/19 talk area (while the next section is locked) or on arriving at floor 40.

## Rewards, amiibo hearts and the record

- **Floor 41:** right after you enter it, the voice speaks the Great Fairy's HD lines and hands out the reward. If you leave floor 41 before that talk (through the portal, or with Withdraw while "Withdraw like HD" is off), the reward is handed out on the way out and arrives once you are back in the world (HD's Withdraw goes back to the "Save and begin" save, so a reward from that visit is undone with everything else); if the talk has not started after 25 seconds of play (time in the pause menu does not count), the reward is given with a notice instead.
  - The first clear gives the next wallet: Big, then Giant, then the **Colossal Wallet** (9,999 rupees). The Colossal Wallet is a flag in the mod's save data, and the `.gci` keeps the Giant Wallet.
  - Later clears give Great Fairy's Tears if you carry an empty bottle.
  - Like in HD, Agitha's wallet rewards move up one size once the cave has given you a wallet.
- **Hearts in the amiibo:** when you answer Yes on the Record screen, the virtual Wolf Link amiibo stores your life at the portal, your heart containers and the floor; the "Cave of Shadows" screen shows them (part hearts included). Scan it on the Collection screen inside the cave to get that life back, once per visit, as in HD: the same amount the plate shows, in quarter hearts, up to your maximum. The Record's "amiibo used" row then shows the amiibo's level ("Level B6"). Saves from before 2.0.0's screen only stored whole hearts; those still heal whole hearts.
- **Cave of Shadows Record:** the Record screen above (time as `h:mm:ss.hh`, max combo = enemies defeated in a row without taking damage, damage received, amiibo used). Like HD it compares with the record the screen wrote last time (the "previous record", kept in the mod's save data). The Mods-window panel still shows the last run, the best full clear and the stored hearts.

## Save safety

- All mod state is in the blob `"cave"` of the Dusklight mod save file `<Card A>/01-GZ2E-gczelda2.mods/local.ricky.hdfeatures.json` (shared with the stamps' blob `"stamps"`). It is written only when the game saves, into the slot being saved. Nothing new is written into the `.gci`.
- A save made inside the cave resumes at your last save point in the world. As in HD, it stores the hearts and rupees you entered the cave with (the rupees you collected count only after a portal exit), because that is what you leave with unless you take a portal. It also stores the form you entered in (a human Link is a wolf inside the cave), so such a save, the Record screen's included, loads with the right form even with the cave or the mod off.
- The mod does nothing outside the vanilla game mode (randomizer, speedrun and other mods' modes).
- Disabling the mod while inside the cave makes you leave it first (as with **Leave the cave**), because the cave's files go away with the mod (during a Withdraw that has already started, the "Save and begin" save is put back at once). Disabling it during the fade into the cave reloads the spot you are standing in instead and undoes what the entry changed (full hearts, the "new path" announcement); the "Saved." menu stays up until that reload removes it. Turning the **Cave of Shadows** switch off inside the cave does the same (the **Leave the cave** warp, keeping the visit), and only then does the switch apply. From the moment the switch is off, no new trip starts (an amiibo scan on a Collection screen that is still open is refused, and a "Cave of Shadows" screen that is open leaves as Cancel as soon as its buttons take input). Turning it back on before the Withdraw has started keeps you in the cave.

## How the files are made, and install

See the repository `README.md` for the install. At the first start, the mod's converter (`mods/hdfeatures/convert`) makes the cave's game files from your TP HD: the `overlay/` files (the `D_SB11` stage files, the scene particles and the HD-only objects, added to the game in memory) and `res/cave/` (`hdtext/` with HD's messages and the four cave flows, `amiibo/` with the scan screen's art, `confirm/` with HD's save-menu layouts for the "Cave of Shadows" and Record screens, and `hd_tags.bin`: the place and size of HD's floor-5/19 talk tags). Its spec is the Python pipeline `tools/build_assets.py`: each file the converter makes is byte for byte the pipeline's (see `BUILDING.md`). With the cave on, the log should show `HD text: 86 messages, flows 1111 1120 1121 1122 installed, 3 talk tag(s)` and `Cave of Shadows ready (0 hook problem(s))`.

**Test on a copy first.** Copy `%APPDATA%\TwilitRealm\Dusklight` and start Dusklight with `--user-dir <copy>`. Autosave writes on every scene change.

## Options (HD Features panel, "Cave of Shadows: options")

Config keys are `mod.local_ricky_hdfeatures.<key>`.

| Option | Key | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cave of Shadows (the switch) | `cave-enabled` | on | The whole feature. Off: no amiibo, no entry, no HD cave text/flows/talk tags, no Colossal Wallet; progress kept. |
| Amiibo button | `cave-amiibo_button` | 0 = Z | The Collection-screen button that scans the virtual amiibo (Z, X or Y). |
| HD sections | `cave-hd_sections` | on | Section gating as described above. |
| Withdraw like HD (back to your save) | `cave-hd_withdraw` | on | Midna's Withdraw ends the visit like HD's reload of the "Save and begin" save: Midna's warp-out, then that save's return place and state, nothing from the visit kept (no reset). Off: like **Leave the cave**, keeping the visit. |
| Start at deepest unlocked section (not in HD) | `cave-start_deepest` | off | Not in HD. On: a visit (and Continue after falling) starts on floor 7 or 21, the first floor of the deepest section unlocked by HD's rules, instead of floor 1. Needs "HD sections". |
| Midna's portal warp | `cave-warp_effect` | on | Midna's portal warp when leaving with Withdraw or "Leave the cave". Off: a plain fade (Withdraw still plays HD's two warp sounds). |
| Midna's warp-out on entry (not in HD) | `cave-entry_warp` | off | Not in HD. On: after "Save and begin" the menu closes and Midna's portal warp takes you in, where GC allows a map warp (HD's way elsewhere). Off: HD's entry, "Saved." held during the fade. Needs "Midna's portal warp". |
| Amiibo prompt | `cave-amiibo_hint` | on | The amiibo prompt on the Collection screen. |
| Floor number notices | `cave-floor_toasts` | off | A notice with the floor number on each new floor (HD has none). |
| Show "Enter the cave now" (debug) | `cave-debug_enter_button` | off | A direct Enter button in the panel. It also appears automatically if the Collection-screen hook fails to install. |

## Without the generated files or hooks

- **No `res/cave/hdtext`:** Midna's menu and the arrival message use the mod's own wording, the scan screens use their own short texts, and there are no section-end or reward talks; the reward uses notices.
- **No `res/cave/amiibo`:** the prompt and scan screen are plain text.
- **No `res/cave/confirm`** (or its pause-menu hooks failed): a successful scan opens the game's save screen directly, without HD's "Cave of Shadows" screen; the trip still starts only after the save. Without the two save-screen hooks, the stock "Saved. Continue playing?" question appears after the save instead of HD's "Saved."; "Yes" starts the trip. Without the hooks that hold "Saved." on screen (`dMw_c::collect_save_move_proc`, and the pause menu's delete hook that lets it go), the menu closes after the save and the stage change follows in the field with a plain fade (logged).
- **No `dStage_Delete` hook:** HD's Withdraw puts the "Save and begin" save back once its stage change's fade has reached black (while the cave scene is being deleted) instead of between the two stages.
- **No Record screen** (its pause-menu hooks failed, or `res/cave/confirm` is missing or older than the Record screen): after the portal's cutscene the screen fades to black and you leave as with **Leave the cave**, with a record notice, and the amiibo stores your hearts by itself. If the screen's files fail to load at black, the record is kept and the save screen opens directly (no amiibo question). Missing portal hooks fall back step by step to the GC portal: no Midna question and an instant fade to the return point.
- **A failed hook** is listed in the panel and only switches off its feature. The one exception is the cutscene message guard: without it the cave's arrival cutscene would crash the game, so entering is refused.

## Known limitations

- **Since the merge into HD Features (2.0.0), the cave was checked in short automated game runs.** It was last play-tested as Cave of Shadows 1.2.1.
- **HD's own code is only partly known.** The section rules (labels 822–829, the tier ALLdie, query 55, the entry bookkeeping, the death-continue, the swc00 timer areas, the Collection-screen notices) were read from HD's `Zelda.rpx` (the developer's notes, not in this repository); so is its amiibo entry check, refusal by refusal. The arrival cutscene's message, the section-end and reward talks follow HD's flows, but the reward talk plays on floor 41 instead of next to the Great Fairy on floor 40, who stays removed.
- **Entering from dungeons, houses, caves, the twilight or while Midna is gravely ill: not play-tested yet** (compiled only). These follow HD's entry check; before this version the mod allowed only field areas.
- **The Sols flag is a stand-in.** HD opens section 3 on its own flag 0x7101, set during Midna's line about the Sols' power going into the sword (HD message 9828) in the Palace of Twilight; GC has no such flag, so the mod uses the Light Sword's item bits, which the same Palace scene sets just before that line.
- **Saves from before this version** keep what they had: a section counted as cleared when you had *reached* floor 6 or 20 (the old rule) stays cleared, and a full clear counts as section 3 cleared. Players who have entered before never hear the first-entry line again.
- **HD's entry from the held "Saved." menu and the Withdraw back to the "Save and begin" save: not play-tested yet** (compiled only). Inferred: that a stage change issued while the pause menu is open runs in Dusklight as it does in HD (GC's play scene issues it without a pause check), that Midna's warp-out plays inside the cave as it does on the overworld, and HD's fade colour (GC's wipe 0 is black). Not reproduced from the reload: the rupee "first time" messages that GC's file load re-arms, and the HUD/oxygen reset of a fresh file load (`dComIfGp_itemDataInit`, not safe between two play scenes); the weather reset (`dKy_clear_game_init`) and the last-scene values are reproduced. An autosave inside the cave does not replace the save Withdraw goes back to (only a pause-menu save does).
- **Exit portal and Record screen: not play-tested yet** (compiled only). Not ported: while the warp cutscene starts, HD sets a camera value to 85.0; that member exists only in HD's camera (HD's `dCamera_c::Reset` clears it at 0x028fe00c, GC's `Reset` has no counterpart), so there is nothing in GC to set. Inferred rather than read from HD: Midna releasing the portal when its switch turns off (HD calls an unnamed Midna function there), the cursor placement on the Record screen's Yes/No buttons and their selection-swap frames (the same as the "Cave of Shadows" screen's), B on the first amiibo question (ignored), the "amiibo used" Wolf Link level (HD stores a value from an unnamed function; the mod uses the amiibo's level), the meaning of HD's second saved value as rupees, and the pacing of the virtual NFC popup.
- **The Record screen's save is the game's own save screen.** HD saves the current Quest Log directly from the Record screen; here the game's save screen opens (pick a log, "Saving...", "Saved."), because Dusklight only stores the mod's save data together with a save made through that screen. It also means B in the log list leaves without a save, which HD does not offer. HD's Record screen also shows Ganondorf and Zelda amiibo rows (both always empty here: there is no virtual Ganondorf or Zelda amiibo) and a Hero Mode mark (GC has no Hero Mode).
- **The amiibo prompt and scan screens are RmlUi overlays**, not HD's 2D layout: the prompt is a short notice in the corner, the scan screen sits in a Dusklight dialog frame. (The "Cave of Shadows" screen after the scan is HD's own layout, drawn by the game.)
- **The "Cave of Shadows" screen and the save screen are two screens here.** In HD they are one (the save menu's mode 6), so the Quest Log list slides in under the same title; here the screen fades to black and the game's own GC save screen fades in. Like HD, the stage then changes straight from the save screen. The selection-swap animation frames, the cursor placement and the music volume are inferred. Mouse input is not supported on this screen and its animations step at the game's 30 Hz (Dusklight's menu pointer and frame smoothing are not available to mods). Dusklight shows notices one at a time, so the prompt waits for the mod's own notices (it is skipped until they have gone) and can be held back briefly by the game's own (the autosave icon, achievements).
- **Disabling the mod or the Cave of Shadows switch** turns the Colossal Wallet back into a Giant Wallet. Rupees above that cap (2,000 with Dusklight's bigger wallets, 1,000 without) are lost on the next rupee change.
- **Particles fit a fixed heap.** The scene particle file is trimmed to the particles the cave's actors reference, so it fits Dusklight's fixed particle heap. Seven HD-only twilight textures are stored at half size.
- **GC gate models.** Gates use the GC model (`SDGate`) instead of HD's gate models, and HD polygon offsets are dropped, so some decals may flicker.
- **The next floor loads early.** It is loaded fully, as in the GC Cave of Ordeals, so its enemies are created one floor early.
- **White Wolfos (floor 28)** use GC behaviour; HD forces their snow behaviour in the cave.
- **Rest-floor chests** use unique chest numbers 60, 61 and 62 (HD's three share number 63, so in HD the floor-20 chest of a run that already opened floor 6's is empty). Like in HD, leaving through a portal or choosing Continue after a game over refills them for the next run.
